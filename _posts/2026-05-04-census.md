Today, Canada kicks off the [2026 Census](https://www.census.gc.ca/en/index). It's the first time I'll be filling it out in my own place, first time doing it without my family. I'll be honest, I'm a little excited and wouldn't mind getting the long form.

It's also the first time I'll fill out the census since I've transitioned. There are a couple questions about gender; Statistics Canada talked about how they improved
the ordering and wording for 2026 [here](https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2026/ref/98-20-0004/2026001/98-20-00042026001-eng.cfm). But overall, I'm looking forward to it. Being part of the numbers to show that we're here, we're not a blip, and we aren't going away. There are rights we need to protect, and so much more to fight for. Pride can't be stopped that easily. If I can contribute to the data that helps to drive real change, I absolutely will.

In a weird twist, I'm proud to be a statistic.
