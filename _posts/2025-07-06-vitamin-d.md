My homepage says this is "where I blog about life, tech, and everything else," but I don't know when I'll get to that
second part. I guess I've been focused on it and **everything else** for so long, and now I'm finally getting around to
the **life** part. There's a lot I've ~~missed out on~~ deferred, and need to get caught up on.

I do actually have a tech-related post, but it's been in the drafts for months with no progress. One day I might get
around to it.

> So, what is this?

I wanna talk about the stuff I've been up to! I send a lot of stuff to friends, but I think it'd be fun to share a bit
here, too.

## Activities 🧗🏾‍♀️

I'm trying out new stuff. I did a bouldering technique class last week. I mean, I picked up a few things and learned,
but I don't think I'm gonna be a regular. I don't have the arm strength (but that's nothing new 😶‍🌫️), and I don't think
I'll commit to it.

## Socializing™

I feel like this is either going to be a mild shock, or painfully obvious, to people that know me IRL. But I went
clubbing recently, for _the second time in my life_ (the first was only a year ago). And it's been fun, I love talking
to people and just enjoying the vibes. I'm genuinely an extrovert, so it's nice to finally express that part of myself.

I've also been going to more casual events, like comedy clubs and random meetups. Pride Month was a great way to find
stuff, and to just start getting out there.
